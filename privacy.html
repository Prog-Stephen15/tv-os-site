Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

TV OS is a personal, non-commercial TV interface that runs on your own computer connected to your TV. This page explains what it does with your information when you sign in with Google. In short: your information stays on your TV, and TV OS does not send it anywhere except back to Google.

What TV OS asks Google for

When you choose Settings → Google account on the TV, Google asks you to allow these permissions:

PermissionWhat TV OS uses it for
Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile)To show who is signed in on the TV.
View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly)To show your subscriptions and their new videos on the TV's home screen. Read-only: TV OS never uploads, comments, likes, subscribes or changes anything.

TV OS never sees your Google password. You type it only on Google's own page, on your phone or computer.

What is stored, and where

  • On your TV only: your name, email address, the web address of your profile picture, and a sign-in token from Google that lets the TV stay signed in. They are kept in one file on the TV, readable only by the TV's user account.
  • In memory only: the short-lived access token and the YouTube information shown on screen. They are not written to disk and disappear when TV OS closes.
  • Nowhere else: TV OS has no server of its own, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your information is only exchanged directly between your TV and Google.

Sharing

TV OS does not sell, rent, share or transfer your information to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI or machine-learning models. People in the same room can see what is on the TV screen, such as your name, picture and YouTube subscriptions.

TV OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

TV OS uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The YouTube app that opens from TV OS is YouTube's own and signs you in separately.

Removing your information

  • On the TV: Settings → Google account → Sign out. This deletes the stored file and asks Google to cancel the sign-in token.
  • From any device: remove TV OS at myaccount.google.com/connections. The TV is signed out the next time it contacts Google.

Children

TV OS is not directed at children. Google's own rules about who can have a Google account apply to signing in.

Changes

If TV OS starts using your information in a new way, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the TV OS site's GitHub page.
