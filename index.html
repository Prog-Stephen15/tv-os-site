TV OS

A personal TV interface for a small PC connected to a TV: boots straight to a home screen you use with a simple remote.

What it is

TV OS is a hobby project: a minimal Linux system with its own full-screen home screen, made to be smooth and easy to use from the couch. It plays your own videos and music, opens apps such as YouTube, and runs on hardware you own. It is not a commercial product and has no ads.

Google sign-in

You can sign in with your Google account on the TV, using Google's sign-in for TVs: the TV shows a code and you approve on your phone. TV OS uses this to show your name and picture and to read your YouTube subscriptions so it can show them on the home screen. It never sees your password.

  • Read-only access to YouTube. TV OS never posts, likes, subscribes or changes anything.
  • Your sign-in stays on your TV. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Sign out on the TV, or remove TV OS at myaccount.google.com/connections.
